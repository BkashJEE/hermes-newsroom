# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
people.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This breaks when the provider times
  out" is useful; "this is terrible" is not.
- Accept that maintainer time is limited and some requests will be declined on
  scope. A decline is not a judgement of you.
- Respect the project's stated rules in `CONTRIBUTING.md` rather than reopening
  them in every thread.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/BkashJEE/hermes-newsroom/security/advisories/new),
which is private to the maintainer, or by a direct message on GitHub. Reports are
handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block accounts. This is a
small personal project, not a foundation with an ethics committee: expect a
human, sometimes slow, response.

This document is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
but is written for this project and is not a copy of it.
